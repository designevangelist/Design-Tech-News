By Adedapo Adesanya Banks, fintechs, and other financial institutions could face a minimum fine of N1 billion for transferring payment transaction data outside Nigeria under a proposed bill before the House of Representatives. The bill also proposes a 10-year prison term and a minimum fine of N500 million for individuals who transfer strategic payment data []
