Endeavor Nigeria has announced the selection of Desmond Koney, founder and CEO of Ghanaian agri-tech startup Complete Farmer, into its network, following a multi-stage selection process culminating at Endeavor’s International Selection Panel in Athens, Greece. Complete Farmer is an agricultural trading company connecting international commodity buyers with smallholder farmers across Ghana and Togo
