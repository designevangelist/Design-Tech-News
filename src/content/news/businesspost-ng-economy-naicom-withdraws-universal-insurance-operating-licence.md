By Aduragbemi Omiyale The operating licence of Universal Insurance Plc has been withdrawn by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM). This action was taken by the regulator over the failure of the underwriting firm, which is listed on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) Limited, to meet the new recapitalisation requirements on or before July 31, 2026.
