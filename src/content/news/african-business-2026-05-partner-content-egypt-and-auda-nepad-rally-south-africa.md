Senior business leaders, diplomats and policymakers gathered at the Egyptian Embassy in Pretoria this week as Egypt and AUDA-NEPAD intensified efforts to mobilise South African participation in the upcoming Alamein Africa Forum, set to take place in Cairo from 25 to 27 June 2026 on the margins of the African Union Mid-Year Coordination Summit.
