Nigerian payments infrastructure company Payaza has acquired Seesail Technologies, the Ghana-founded commerce platform for small businesses. Payaza offers payment collection, payout and merchant tools to businesses in Africa and international markets. It handles card, mobile money and bank payments in more than 40 currencies and operates in more than 27 countries. Disrupt Africa reported recently
