In a major disruption to South Africa’s institutional investment market, Absa has ousted Standard Bank to become the Master Custodian for the Government Employees’ Pension Fund (GEPF). With assets exceeding $218 billion (R3.5 trillion), the GEPF stands as Africa’s largest pension fund, supporting over 1.2 million active members and more than 565,000 pensioners and beneficiaries.
