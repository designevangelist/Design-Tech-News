Renewable energy technology company Okra Solar has secured a $7 million debt financing facility from British International Investment (BII) to expand electricity connections in underserved rural communities across Nigeria. The financing will support the procurement of solar equipment, batteries, and other essential components needed to accelerate the deployment of the companys mesh-grid technology
