Kenyan prosecutors have reversed their decision to charge four FlexPay executives, finding that Naivas’s KES 31.2 million ($240,000) claim is a civil dispute. The case follows the collapse of a five-year partnership that processed more than KES 3 billion in payments from Naivas shoppers.
