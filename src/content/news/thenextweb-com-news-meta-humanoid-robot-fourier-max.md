Meta has built a prototype humanoid robot with Chinese robotics company Fourier in about three months, and the two are already working on a second version. Jyoti Mann reported the project for The Information on Saturday, citing people familiar with it. The robot is known inside Meta as Max and weighs about 48kg (106 pounds).
