Tanzanian e-health startup Medikea has raised US$1.2 million in pre-seed funding as it opens two new clinics and targets a network of 10 facilities by the end of next year. Founded in 2022, Medikea is a vertically integrated healthcare company that combines 24/7 virtual consultations with its own network of clinics, pharmacies and diagnostic labs.
