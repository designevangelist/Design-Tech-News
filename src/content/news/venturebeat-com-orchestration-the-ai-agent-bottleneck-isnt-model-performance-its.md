Enterprise AI agents are stalling — not because of model performance, but because of permissioning. Every agentic workflow eventually hits the same wall: what is this agent allowed to touch, on whose behalf, and how does the system know? Workday&#x27;s answer is to make its existing system of record the governance layer for agents. Gerrit Kazmaier, the company&#x27;s president for product and tech
