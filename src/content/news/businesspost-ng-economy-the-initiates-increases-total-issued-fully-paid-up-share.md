By Aduragbemi Omiyale The total issued and fully paid-up share capital of The Initiates has been increased by 125 per cent following the additional listing of the company’s equities on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) Limited on Wednesday. During the midweek trading session, the firm brought an additional 1,110,018,448 ordinary shares to Customs Street for listing. []
