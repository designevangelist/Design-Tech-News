Australian mining major Northern Star Resources has unanimously rejected a confidential, unsolicited takeover bid from South African gold producer Gold Fields. The proposed acquisition, valuing Northern Star at approximately AU$38.7 billion (R445.3 billion), was dismissed by the Australian firms board as a highly opportunistic attempt to capitalize on recent market vulnerabilities.
