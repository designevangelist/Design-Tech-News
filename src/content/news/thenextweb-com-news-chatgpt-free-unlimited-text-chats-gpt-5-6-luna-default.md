OpenAI is removing usage limits on text chats for free ChatGPT users, the company announced on Thursday. The change means free users can send unlimited text-only messages without hitting a daily cap. Limits on image generation, file uploads, and voice tools will still apply. The company is also switching the default model for all users [&hellip;] This story continues at The Next Web
