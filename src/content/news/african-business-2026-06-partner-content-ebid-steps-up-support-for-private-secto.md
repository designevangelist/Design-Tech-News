The ECOWAS Bank for Investment and Development (EBID) has approved financing packages worth more than USD 75m and EUR 105m to strengthen energy security, expand access to finance for SMEs and boost industrial development across the region. The investments reflect the Bank's growing focus on catalysing private sector-led growth under its new five-year strategy.
