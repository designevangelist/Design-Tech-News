Ugandan startup XenFi is helping internet service providers (ISPs) and Wi-Fi hotspot operators sell internet access, manage customers and collect payments via mobile money from a single platform. Founded in 2025, XenFi has built a hotspot billing and management platform that also lets operators monitor transactions and manage multiple MikroTik routers from a centralised dashboard.
