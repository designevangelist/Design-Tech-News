By Dipo Olowookere The Nigerian Exchange (NGX) Limited rebounded by 0.05 per cent on Thursday following renewed buying interest in Unilever Nigeria, International Breweries, Wema Bank, and others. The upliftment recorded yesterday happened amid weak investor sentiment as the bourse ended with 18 appreciating equities and 45 declining equities, implying a negative market breadth index. []
