The Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) has officially instructed prospective phone buyers across the country to dial short code *315# before purchasing any mobile device. The strategic directive comes as part of a regulatory crackdown against the influx of counterfeit, stolen, and non-compliant hardware into the Nigerian telecommunications ecosystem.
