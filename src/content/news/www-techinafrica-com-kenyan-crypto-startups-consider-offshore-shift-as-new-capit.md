Kenyan cryptocurrency startups are considering moving some operations to Mauritius and South Africa as new licensing requirements impose capital thresholds that early-stage companies say could make it difficult to remain competitive in the local market. The concerns follow the introduction of Kenyas Virtual Asset Service Providers Regulations, 2026, which set licensing and operational requirements
