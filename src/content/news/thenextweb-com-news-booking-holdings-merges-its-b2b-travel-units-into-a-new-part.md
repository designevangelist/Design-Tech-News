Booking Holdings is putting two of its units that sell travel to other firms into one new unit, Booking Partner Division, the company said on Wednesday. The division will start operating in January 2027. It brings together Booking.com Strategic Partnerships and Rocket Travel by Agoda. Omri Morgenshtern, chief executive of Agoda, will run it.
