The African Development Bank Group has approved a €100 million loan to Gotion Power Morocco for the development of Africa’s first integrated lithium iron phosphate battery gigafactory. The facility will be located in the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra Free Trade Zone and is expected to strengthen Morocco’s position in electric mobility, battery manufacturing and green technology value chains.
