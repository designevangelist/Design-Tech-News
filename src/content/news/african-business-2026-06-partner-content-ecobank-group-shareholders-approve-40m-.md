Shareholders of Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI), the parent company of the Ecobank Group, today approved all resolutions tabled at its 2026 Annual General Meeting in Lomé, including the approval of a dividend of US$40 million for the 2025 financial year - the Group's first distribution to shareholders since 2022.
