Nigerian startup ShoppersBag has joined FasterCapital’s EquityPilot programme to help it refine campaign workflows, test in-store tracking integrations, and prepare reporting assets for brand partners. ShoppersBag uses QR-enabled eco-bag tags and mobile and web applications to measure in-store usage, reward eco-conscious shoppers, and connect bag producers and brands for incentive-driven campaigns
