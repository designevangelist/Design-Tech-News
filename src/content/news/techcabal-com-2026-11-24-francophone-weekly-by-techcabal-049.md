Wave is the first mobile money provider to win a licence in the DRC in 11 years. Its free-withdrawal model could put pressure on Airtel Money and M-PESA just as the country prepares to ban cash dollar payments for goods and services. With digital dollar payments still allowed, can Wave make the Congolese franc cheaper to use?
