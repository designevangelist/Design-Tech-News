Applications are now open for the SAB Foundation Tholoana Enterprise Programme, an 18-month business growth programme for entrepreneurs leading commercially proven South African businesses that are either growing or poised for growth. The Tholoana Enterprise Programme, delivered by Fetola, provides tailored support to strengthen leadership, business systems, financial capability and market access.
