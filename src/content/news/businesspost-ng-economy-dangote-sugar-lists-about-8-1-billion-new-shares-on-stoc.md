By Aduragbemi Omiyale About 8.1 billion additional ordinary shares of Dangote Sugar Refinery Plc have been listed on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) Limited. The equities were listed on Customs Street on Monday, September 7, 2026, a notice signed by the Head of the Issuer Regulation Department of the NGX Regulation Limited, Mr Godstime Iwenekhai, stated.
