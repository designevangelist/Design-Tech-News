Nigeria’s new stablecoin regulations are reshaping the economics of issuing digital currencies. With mandatory central bank deposits, tighter investment limits, and stricter reserve requirements, issuers face higher costs and reduced earning potential. Commercial banks, meanwhile, could emerge as the biggest beneficiaries of the new framework.
