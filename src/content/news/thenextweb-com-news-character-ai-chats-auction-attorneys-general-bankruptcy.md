A bipartisan coalition of 42 attorneys general wants a bankruptcy court to stop Character.AI from auctioning users’ private chats. The sale covers more than 21 million US accounts, California Attorney General Rob Bonta said on Tuesday. Of those, more than 2.1 million belong to minors.
