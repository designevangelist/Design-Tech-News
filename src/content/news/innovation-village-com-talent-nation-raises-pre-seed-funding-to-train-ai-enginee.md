Talent Nation, an AI engineering talent development company, has raised pre-seed funding to expand its training programmes and connect African AI engineers with businesses worldwide. The funding round attracted investments from Enza Capital, LocalGlobe, Future Africa, Kaleo Ventures, Ajim Capital, Solving the World’s Greatest Problems and Accelerate Africa. The company did not disclose the amount
