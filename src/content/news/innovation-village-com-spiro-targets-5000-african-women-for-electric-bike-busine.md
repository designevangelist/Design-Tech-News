Electric mobility company Spiro and Entrepreneurial Solutions Partners (ESP) have launched a programme to help 5,000 African women establish businesses using electric motorcycles by 2030. The initiative combines business training, access to electric bikes and connections to commercial platforms, aiming to help more women earn a living in Africa’s growing transport and delivery sectors.
