Cascador, a platform for growth-stage founders building transformative businesses, has selected 10 growth-stage Nigerian companies for its 2026 ScaleUp programme, which equips entrepreneurs with the leadership skills, strategic clarity, and network to succeed. Since 2019, Cascador has supported 70 founders who have collectively raised over $125 million and created almost 67,000 jobs in 2025 alone.
