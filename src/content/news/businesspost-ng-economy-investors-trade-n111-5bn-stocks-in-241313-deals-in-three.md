By Dipo Olowookere The three-day trading sessions of last week witnessed the exchange of 2.398 billion stocks valued at N111.480 billion in 241,313 deals on the floor of the Nigerian Exchange (NGX). In the preceding week, which had five trading days, market participants bought and sold 3.875 billion stocks worth N161.757 billion in 334,745 deals. []
