Moroccan proptech startup Agenz has closed a $5 million seed round to build out the data infrastructure and transaction tools that Moroccos property market has long operated without. The round was co-led by Paris-based venture firm Breega, Attijariwafa Ventures — the corporate investment arm of North Africas largest bank — and pan-African fund Saviu Ventures.
