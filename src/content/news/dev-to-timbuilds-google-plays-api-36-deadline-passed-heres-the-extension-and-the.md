<p>On August 31, 2026, Google Play stopped accepting new apps and app updates that don't target Android 16 (API level 36). If you haven't upgraded yet, you're not reading a warning about the future — your next upload is already blocked.</p> <p>The good news: there is a documented way to buy time. The bad news: it's a one-time extension to November 1, 2026, requested through a form in Play Console.
