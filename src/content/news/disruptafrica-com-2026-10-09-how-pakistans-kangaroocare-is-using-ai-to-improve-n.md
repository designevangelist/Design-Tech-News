Pakistani startup KangarooCare AI is developing intelligent, AI-enabled medical devices that are utilising the latest technologies to improve healthcare outcomes, with a particular focus on neonatal care. Founded by Islam Un Nisa in 2018, KangarooCare has built out a product portfolio that includes neonatal incubators, infant warmers, phototherapy solutions, and AI-enabled monitoring technologies.
