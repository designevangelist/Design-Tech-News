By Aduragbemi Omiyale About N8.96 billion was paid in death claims and annuity benefits to policyholders and beneficiaries by Stanbic IBTC Insurance Limited in 2025, in contrast to the N5.33 billion disbursed in 2024. It was gathered that of this amount, N1.59 billion covered 1,162 death claims, while N7.37 billion went towards 4,824 annuity payments []
